# UI audit — 2026-10-13

Input for the UI, menu, design and connections pass (STATUS "Next" item 0). Method: `apps/web/e2e/ui-audit.mjs`
signs up a fresh workspace, seeds a modest dataset (items, parties, warehouses, a receipt, a work centre and machine, a
calendar, an active BOM, a released work order, a maintenance plan and order), and opens every app page that needs no
other record (97 of 130) at 1400 px and 390 px. It records console errors, failed API calls, horizontal overflow,
headings and screenshots. Code was read where the screenshots raised a question.

## 1. Technical health — good

- 194 page views: **no crashes, no console errors, no failed API calls, every page has a heading**, nothing stuck on
  "Loading…".
- **10 pages scroll sideways on a phone** (390 px): new journal, new sandbox exercise, stock entries, the maintenance
  order page, the work order page, units & HSN, new invoice / sales order / quotation, roles. Cause: wide button rows
  and line tables that do not wrap or scroll inside their card.

## 2. Menu — too long, and grouped by when things were built

- **72 links in 11 always-open sections** (Operations 9, Services 9, Quality 7, Sales 4, Buying 6, Inventory 6, Masters 3,
  Finance 12, Maintenance 3, Plant 3, Settings 9). On a laptop most of it is below the fold.
- Related things are split: machines live under Plant, their work centres and calendars under Operations, their
  maintenance under Maintenance, their taps under Plant → Devices; incoming inspection sits under Buying while all
  other inspections are under Quality; GST/ITC-04 appear under Finance while their connections are under Settings.
- Finance pages repeat their 12 links again as a row of blue links under every page title.
- Nothing remembers which sections a person uses; a machinist sees the same long list (trimmed by permission only).

## 3. Home — a placeholder

- Shows a setup checklist, an empty "Waiting on me" that says approvals "will appear here from Phase 1", an e-invoice
  card, and a **development roadmap** ("Next: sales and tax invoices", which shipped long ago).
- Nothing about the business: no open orders, overdue invoices, stock to reorder, machines down, maintenance due,
  inspections waiting, or the person's own tasks.

## 4. Page layout — mostly consistent, with drift

- Shared building blocks are used well (`@factoryos/ui` cards, tables, badges, one colour set; only one hard-coded colour
  found).
- **Primary action placement varies:** 52 pages put "New …" at the top right of the header; others put it under the
  description on the left (BOMs, calendars, CAPA, NCR, skill matrix, maintenance plans, rate cards, projects, billing
  runs, memberships, subscriptions, timesheets, bookings, campaigns).
- **Empty states vary:** 53 places use the illustrated empty state; about 49 show a plain sentence inside an empty table
  ("No vouchers yet…").
- **Draft forms** use a small breadcrumb and a monospace "New purchase order" heading; other create pages use the normal
  page heading.
- Long, technical page descriptions in places (Finance: "INR ledgers retain six decimal places; document totals keep
  their original precision").
- The sidebar's white background stops at the first screen height on long pages.

## 5. Forms

- Date fields show the browser's US format (mm/dd/yyyy) instead of DD-MM-YYYY.
- Narrow selects truncate (GST % shows "Fr").
- Line tables on sales/purchase forms do not adapt to phones.

## 6. Connections between records — the biggest gap

- **No page for an item, a customer or a supplier.** You cannot open "Pump housing" and see its stock, BOMs, work
  orders, open orders, invoices, quality records; or open a customer and see its quotes, orders, invoices, receipts and
  outstanding.
- **Search** ("Search or jump to…") finds pages only, not records (no document numbers, items, parties, batches,
  serials).
- Document pages link to their source in places (receipt → PO, invoice → order) but not consistently both ways (e.g.
  from a purchase order to its receipts and invoices, from a work order to its job work and inspections).
- External connections (GST portal/NIC, e-mail, storage, taps, printers, Tally) each have their own settings page in a
  different section, with no single place that shows what is connected and whether it works.

## 7. Code health affecting the UI

- 14 components are written as single very long lines (for example bank reconciliation, GST sandbox, supplier return
  forms, the e-mail delivery table), which makes consistent changes to them slow and risky.
- Stale developer text visible to users (Home roadmap, "from Phase 1").

## Proposed direction (for the design, pending the user's choices)

1. A shorter, task-grouped menu with collapsible sections and favourites/recent, aligned with how people work.
2. A real Home: what needs my attention, by role, from live data.
3. Record pages for items, customers/suppliers (and links both ways between related documents) plus record search.
4. One page pattern: header with actions on the right, one empty state, one draft-form header, phone-safe tables.
5. A "Connections" page listing every external integration and its health.
6. Fix the 10 phone overflows, date format, truncated selects, sidebar background, and stale text.
